About This Feed

HiEd Cyber News collects cybersecurity news for college and university IT and security teams: campus breaches and ransomware, ghost-student and financial-aid fraud, GLBA and FERPA compliance, and EDUCAUSE and REN-ISAC updates. The feed is rebuilt automatically every 30 minutes.

Threat Watch stories aren't about higher ed. They cover attacks and vulnerabilities in technology campuses commonly run, such as Microsoft 365, Ellucian, Canvas, Fortinet, Ivanti and VMware, and CISA's actively exploited list.
